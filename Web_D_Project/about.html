Welcome to Our Marriage Lawn & Hotel

Established in May 2024 under the vision of Mahendra Pratap Pandey, our Marriage Lawn & Hotel is your destination for elegant events and comfortable stays. Nestled in a peaceful location, we offer a perfect setting for weddings, receptions, and other special occasions.

Our key facilities include:

  • Spacious Banquet Hall – Ideal for all types of events and gatherings.
  • Comfortable Guest Rooms – Well-appointed for a pleasant stay.
  • Walking Area – Beautifully landscaped for relaxation and leisure.
  • Ample Parking Space – Convenient and secure for all guests.

We are committed to providing excellent service and a welcoming atmosphere to make your celebrations truly memorable. We look forward to hosting you!
